# Manuscript revision — 27 October 2026

The main manuscript and Supplementary Methods were revised from the September 2026 author drafts. Original documents and the older tracked manuscript were preserved.

## Changes

- Describes exactly one selected cell detector: CellViT++ (default) or StarDist. The current pipeline does not run detector consensus or a HoVer-Net branch. StarDist nuclear instances do not carry invented CellViT classifier labels.
- Describes the full-slide CellViT++ SAM-H route and eight separate classifier views: PanNuke, CoNSeP, Lizard, NuCLS main, NuCLS super, Ocelot, MIDOG and PanopTILs. Common mask/table export preserves registration and cell identifiers. Automatic final-manifest registration across both detector formats remains an explicit limitation.
- Distinguishes calibrated 224 × 224-pixel Virchow2 tissue observations at 0.5 µm/pixel from CellViT image regions and per-cell vectors.
- Describes the refined Bora continuation: global prediction field, native H&E refinement, retained exact mask and compact shared boundaries. Separately frozen Neighbor-context PLS assigns optional tissue identities. Geometry fidelity guards are not independent biological accuracy measurements.
- Replaces Figure 1 with the current two-choice workflow. Figures 2–4 retain their original image bytes and now identify their archived implementation context.
- Separates current CPU/dataflow verification from archived inference and biological results. GigaTIME remains optional and its virtual markers are distinguished from measured proteins.

## Evidence boundaries

The numerical breast-cancer results remain tied to source commit `c0b94b6e0e3b4ff285647033566d7fbfb34665ed` and runtime `ghcr.io/tkcaccia/cellphenotyper-runtime:2.7-gpu-amd64`. In particular, the 279,899 consensus-cell result belongs to the archived implementation; it is not a result of the new detector choice. Historical transfer failures and virtual-marker seams remain disclosed.

The completed PM016 full-slide record is not presented as a new breast-cancer comparison or independent accuracy experiment. Updated end-to-end inference, matched molecular references and held-out expert annotations are still required before new accuracy or throughput claims. No new runtime image was built for this revision.
