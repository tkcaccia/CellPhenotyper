# GigaTIME Virtual-Marker Validation Protocol

## Purpose and claim boundary

This protocol evaluates whether GigaTIME values generated by CellPhenotyper agree with independently measured, spatially registered protein signals. It is separate from seam QC: seam QC tests image continuity, whereas this study tests biological concordance.

The output remains a **virtual marker score**. Correlation does not establish assay interchangeability, measurement calibration, diagnostic validity or clinical utility. The released GigaTIME model is research-only and generates 21 protein channels plus two background channels from H&E patches. Its original study used paired H&E and mIF data, but transfer to a new site, tissue, scanner or staining process requires local and genuinely external validation.

## Technical nuclear gate before cell-marker analysis

The pipeline runs `bin/gigatime_nuclear_qc.py` on the persisted, native GigaTIME output. The DAPI plane and an independently generated H&E StarDist nuclear mask must occupy the same physical region; source-mask pixels are mapped to the calibrated 0.25 µm/pixel inference grid. The stage checks model-grid metadata and dimensions before scoring. It emits a JSON report and an image with StarDist outlines over virtual DAPI. The production `fail` mode prevents the GigaTIME channel and integrated quantification from entering later workflow stages when this check fails. Previously written integrated quantification files from a failed run remain **unvalidated** and must not be reported as accepted output.

The score combines (i) AUC for DAPI inside StarDist nuclei versus a nearby extranuclear ring, (ii) Dice at a prevalence-matched DAPI threshold, and (iii) DAPI contrast immediately inside minus immediately outside nuclear outlines. A separate edge-to-core nuclear signal ratio screens for dark image borders. The composite alignment score is `clip(0.5 × (2 × AUC − 1) + 0.25 × Dice + 0.25 × clip(boundary_contrast / 0.20, 0, 1), 0, 1)`. Default gates are AUC ≥ 0.70, Dice ≥ 0.35, boundary contrast ≥ 0.05 on the 0–1 score scale, and edge ratio ≥ 0.65 when enough edge nuclei exist. These are **provisional technical thresholds**, not a clinical or biological validation. The separate GigaTIME seam check also remains required. A failed score may reflect an incorrect nuclear mask, domain shift, or incorrect virtual DAPI; the overlay must be inspected.

In one calibrated MoNuSAC prostate ROI, the source was 0.252 µm/pixel and GigaTIME ran at exactly 0.25 µm/pixel. After fixing overlap-blend normalization, a bounded Chiamaka case produced an edge ratio of 1.148 but failed the StarDist nuclear check: AUC 0.668, Dice 0.636 and boundary contrast 0.016. The case used 70 StarDist nuclei. This establishes that the earlier dark border was a software artifact while **cell-level virtual DAPI remains unverified** on this image. The [side-by-side images and native-store report](https://tkcaccia.github.io/Tambua-Analysis/#dapi-qc) are public. Cell-type accuracy measured from the earlier virtual markers is historical until the cohort is rerun with a passing technical gate and a measured-marker validation.

The exact CNN checkpoint used in that case was previously tested on 45 held-out paired H&E/mIF patches released with GigaTIME: mean nuclear AUC was 0.920 at the authors' 0.25 µm/pixel inference scale. Patient independence of those patches is unknown, and they do not validate MoNuSAC transfer. A separate bounded Chiamaka diagnostic held the MoNuSAC image, checkpoint, physical scale and StarDist mask fixed while applying two stain transfers from five released H&E reference patches. Raw, Macenko and tissue-Reinhard inputs yielded nuclear AUC 0.668, 0.625 and 0.586, respectively; all failed the technical gate. These two simple color transfers are therefore not a fix for this field. The [public diagnostic images and report](https://tkcaccia.github.io/Tambua-Analysis/#dapi-qc) retain the negative results. No stain transfer is enabled in the production pipeline on this evidence.

Relevant evidence:

- GigaTIME primary report: Valanarasu et al., *Cell* (2026), DOI `10.1016/j.cell.2025.11.016`.
- Model card and research-use limitation: `https://huggingface.co/prov-gigatime/GigaTIME`.
- A 2026 independent external-validation preprint reports that immune-marker concordance can collapse after controlling for cellularity. This is not yet peer reviewed, but it motivates mandatory cellularity-adjusted results rather than reliance on unadjusted correlations alone: `https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7224105`.

## Study design

1. Split patients, never cells or tiles, into development, internal-test and genuinely external-test cohorts.
2. Lock registration, normalization, matching, marker thresholds, exclusion rules, endpoints and acceptance margins before opening either test split.
3. Represent every attempted specimen, including failed registration, missing assay channels and failed pipeline runs, in the study accounting. The metric manifest contains only rows that passed the prespecified registration gate; failures belong in the CONSORT-style accounting table and denominator.
4. Include prespecified tissue compartments and failure-enriched strata: low cellularity, necrosis, blur, folds, stain extremes, compression, weak expression and high background.
5. Use an immutable CellPhenotyper commit, container digest, GigaTIME revision/checkpoint hash and parameter file.
6. Estimate sample size from a confidence-interval width or a prespecified equivalence/noninferiority margin at the patient level. Cells cannot determine the independent sample size.

## Reference and registration

- Cell-level evaluation requires same-section or otherwise physically paired H&E/mIF with a defensible one-to-one cell mapping. Serial sections must not be presented as cell-level ground truth.
- Serial-section data may be evaluated only at prespecified fixed spatial-region level, with `observation_unit=spatial_bin` and `reference_design=serial_section_region_level`.
- Registration must be locked without viewing GigaTIME output, use at least three independent landmarks, and record median and 95th-percentile physical error.
- `registration_p95_error_um` must not exceed the prespecified `registration_acceptance_p95_um`.
- Record the fraction of eligible observations that were matched. `matched_fraction` must meet the locked `minimum_matched_fraction`; unmatched observations cannot be silently removed.
- Marker measurement, background subtraction, spillover compensation, segmentation and normalization must be defined by the reference-assay protocol, not chosen to improve GigaTIME agreement.

## Paired table

Use `resources/virtual_marker_validation_manifest.template.csv`. Each manifest row identifies one marker, condition and ROI and points to a matched CSV table. The paired table requires:

- a unique matched-observation ID;
- crop level-0 `x` and `y` coordinates within the declared image bounds;
- one GigaTIME score column;
- one independently measured reference column;
- for non-DAPI markers, one or more prespecified cellularity-control columns separated by semicolons in the manifest.

Recommended controls include measured DAPI, nuclear area or a prespecified local nuclear-density quantity. Do not use a control selected from the locked-test association. Controls do not prove absence of all confounding.

Use `condition` for immutable model or preprocessing variants. Do not mix model revisions, physical scales or normalization procedures under one label.

## Endpoints

Recommended primary endpoint: patient-macro-averaged, cellularity-adjusted Spearman correlation for a prespecified marker and observation unit in the external-test cohort.

Secondary continuous endpoints:

- unadjusted Pearson and Spearman correlation;
- cellularity-adjusted Pearson and Spearman correlation;
- percentile-rank mean absolute error;
- RMSE after independent z standardization;
- fixed-region spatial Pearson and Spearman correlation;
- top-quantile spatial-hotspot Jaccard overlap.

Optional binary endpoints require both reference and GigaTIME thresholds to be locked on development data:

- AUROC and average precision;
- sensitivity, specificity, positive and negative predictive value, and F1;
- reference and predicted prevalence;

GigaTIME values are required to remain on `[0,1]`, but the benchmark does not report a probability-calibration score because the current pipeline treats them as uncalibrated virtual-marker scores.

Report every marker. Correct for multiplicity or explicitly label secondary marker analyses exploratory. DAPI should be shown as a morphology-linked positive control, but good DAPI performance cannot validate immune-marker biology.

## Execution

```bash
python bin/benchmark_virtual_markers.py \
  --manifest virtual_marker_validation_manifest.csv \
  --outdir virtual_marker_validation_results \
  --bootstrap-replicates 2000 \
  --spatial-bin-um 64 \
  --hotspot-quantile 0.90 \
  --seed 2026
```

The tool fails on patient leakage, cell-level serial-section claims, incomplete or post hoc thresholds, non-passing registration, insufficient registration landmarks, registration error above the locked gate, inadequate match fraction, absent cellularity controls, duplicate pair IDs, coordinate-frame violations, excessive missingness or oversized tables.

## Outputs

- `virtual_marker_metrics_per_roi.csv`: complete-case accounting, registration/matching metadata, continuous, binary and spatial metrics per ROI.
- `virtual_marker_metrics_per_patient.csv`: ROI-macro-averaged patient results; this is the inferential level.
- `virtual_marker_metrics_aggregate.csv`: patient-macro estimates and patient bootstrap confidence intervals.
- `virtual_marker_metrics_by_stratum.csv`: descriptive site, scanner, tissue, compartment and quality results.
- `discordant_observations.geojson`: the largest within-ROI percentile-rank disagreements for blinded review; this is not an exclusion list.
- `virtual_marker_provenance.csv`: table hashes, model/reference identities, column mappings and row accounting.
- `virtual_marker_benchmark_summary.json`: frozen evaluation contract and claim limitation.
- `virtual_marker_benchmark_report.html`: review-first summary emphasizing adjusted and spatial evidence.

## Acceptance and reporting

The protocol must name the primary marker, direction, acceptable effect or margin, missing-data handling and external cohort before testing. Report patient-level distributions and intervals, not only pooled cell-level correlations. Show the change from unadjusted to cellularity-adjusted association, registration and matching failures, prevalence, discordance examples and all attempted markers.

A failed marker must remain visible. A technically smooth GigaTIME OME-TIFF, plausible tissue overlay or association with a downstream cluster is not a substitute for measured-protein concordance.
